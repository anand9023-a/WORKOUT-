4-Day Workout Plan

Equipment: Bodyweight, Pull-up Bar & 3kg Dumbbells

Execution Rules
  • Tempo: 4 seconds down (slow eccentric) on every single rep to maximize 3kg dumbbell tension.
  • Intensity: Push every set close to failure.
  • Diet: High protein and caloric surplus are mandatory for growth.
